# AI-Powered Digital Transformation Strategy

A strategy case for a fictional healthcare provider, MedCare. The project outlines a digital-transformation roadmap that connects workflow redesign with analytics and AI-enabled support.

## Scope

- Map administrative and patient-service bottlenecks.
- Prioritize digital workflow and analytics opportunities.
- Develop a phased implementation roadmap and financial case.
- Identify operational, adoption and delivery risks.

## Main deliverable

- `consulting_reports-4.pdf` — transformation strategy and business case.

The report models a 71% reduction in waiting time, ₹12 Cr in administrative savings, satisfaction increasing from 69 to 89, and an estimated 182% ROI over three years. These are scenario projections in the case report; they are not measured or deployed outcomes.

## Project status

This is a report-based strategy case, not a deployed healthcare system. Keep the fictional MedCare context and projection qualifiers with any of its results.
